🤖 AI Job Market Insights 2025–2026
🚀 Project Snapshot
Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze global AI job market trends using Python, SQL, and Power BI. This project transforms raw job posting data into meaningful insights on hiring trends, salary distributions, in-demand skills, experience requirements, industries, and geographic demand to support data-driven career and business decisions.

📁 Project Overview
The AI industry is evolving rapidly, creating demand for professionals with the right technical and analytical skills. This project explores thousands of AI job postings from 2025–2026 to answer critical business and career questions through data analytics and interactive visualization.

Questions Answered
Which countries have the highest AI hiring demand?
Which AI skills are most requested by employers?
Which industries are hiring AI professionals the most?
How do salaries vary across countries and experience levels?
Which job roles are currently in highest demand?
What hiring trends can be observed across the AI market?

🎯 Business Objectives
✅ Analyze global AI hiring trends
✅ Identify the most in-demand AI skills
✅ Compare salary distributions across countries
✅ Discover industry-wise hiring patterns
✅ Understand experience-level requirements
✅ Build an interactive dashboard for easy exploration

📌 Key Findings

✅ AI hiring demand is concentrated in a few major global markets.
✅ Machine Learning, Python, SQL, and Generative AI are among the most requested skills.
✅ Salary levels vary significantly across countries and industries.
✅ Experience requirements differ depending on job role and specialization.
✅ Interactive filtering enables detailed exploration of hiring trends across multiple dimensions.
